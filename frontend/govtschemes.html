🏛 Government Agricultural Subsidies & Schemes

Official Central & State Farming Welfare Portals

🔍

🌱 PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in 3 equal installments to eligible farmer families.

Official Application Portal ↗

🛡 Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest.

Official Portal ↗

💧 Sub-Mission on Agriculture Mechanization (SMAM)

Subsidies up to 40%-50% for purchasing tractors, tillers, rotavators, and modern farm machinery.

Official Portal ↗
